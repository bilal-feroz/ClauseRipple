# CR-095 — Halcyon Vector retention window change

## Context

Halcyon Vector reduced its post-deletion retention window from thirty to fourteen days.

## Decision

No customer action is required because the shorter retention window reduces exposure. The Vector Index Service runbook was updated to reference the fourteen-day window.
