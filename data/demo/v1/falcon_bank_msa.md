# Master Services Agreement — Falcon Bank

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between AtlasAI FZ-LLC ("AtlasAI") and Falcon Bank ("Customer").

## Schedule A — Subscribed Services

- DocSummarizer (Enterprise tier, 2,500 seats)

## 7. Data Protection

### 7.1 Definitions

"Customer Data" means all documents, prompts and outputs submitted to the Services by or
on behalf of the Customer.

### 7.2 Data Residency

AtlasAI shall ensure that Customer Data is stored and processed only in the following regions: the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and the European Union (EU). Any processing outside these regions requires the Customer's prior written consent.

### 7.4 Change Notification

AtlasAI shall notify the Customer in writing at least thirty (30) days before any change to the locations in which Customer Data is processed.

## 12. Term

This Agreement continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months.
