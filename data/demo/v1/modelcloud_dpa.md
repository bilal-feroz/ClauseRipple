# Data Processing Addendum — ModelCloud

This Data Processing Addendum is entered into between ModelCloud ("Processor") and
AtlasAI FZ-LLC ("Controller") and forms part of the ModelCloud Enterprise Agreement.

## 4. Processing Locations

### 4.1 Permitted regions

ModelCloud may process Covered Customer Data in the following regions: the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and the European Union (EU).

### 4.2 Sub-processors

ModelCloud will give the Controller thirty (30) days' notice before engaging a new
sub-processor that processes Covered Customer Data.

## 5. Security

ModelCloud maintains ISO 27001 certification and encrypts Covered Customer Data in
transit and at rest.
