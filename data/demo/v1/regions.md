# Data Region Reference

AtlasAI classifies every processing location into one of the following data regions.
Contracts, vendor agreements and architecture documents must use these names.

| Code | Region | Aliases |
|---|---|---|
| UAE | United Arab Emirates | UAE, United Arab Emirates |
| EU | European Union | EU, European Union |
| US | United States | US, USA, United States |
