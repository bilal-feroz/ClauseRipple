# Government Services Agreement — GulfGov

This Government Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between AtlasAI FZ-LLC ("AtlasAI") and GulfGov ("Customer").

## Schedule A — Subscribed Services

- LocalSearch (Sovereign tier, dedicated deployment)

## 9. Sovereignty

### 9.1 Sovereign Data Residency

Customer Data shall be stored and processed only in the following regions: the United Arab Emirates (UAE). No Customer Data, metadata or model outputs may leave the United Arab Emirates.

### 9.3 Change Notification

AtlasAI shall notify the Customer at least sixty (60) days before any change to the locations in which Customer Data is processed.

## 15. Term

This Agreement continues for an initial term of sixty (60) months.
