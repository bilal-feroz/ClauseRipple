This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between AtlasAI FZ-LLC ("AtlasAI") and Acme Insurance ("Customer").

## 8. Data Protection

### 8.3 Data Residency

Customer Data shall be processed only in the following regions: the United Arab Emirates (UAE) and the European Union (EU). AtlasAI shall not transfer Customer Data to any third country without a written amendment to this Agreement.

### 8.5 Change Notification

AtlasAI shall notify the Customer at least forty-five (45) days before any change to the locations in which Customer Data is processed.

## 14. Term

This Agreement continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months.
